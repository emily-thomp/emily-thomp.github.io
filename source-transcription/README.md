# Source transcription

The book was organized from 39 supplied scan images and two existing OCR runs
(PPStructureV3 and Qwen). The public site contains eight Chinese chapters and
eight English translations. Original scan images and supplied figures are in
`docs/assets/`. Scan 39 contains catalogue metadata rather than book content.

`manifest.json` records source-image hashes; `corrections.json` records targeted
OCR changes; `formulas.json` retains the extracted LaTeX. The edition remains
an initial corrected OCR transcription: text has not received complete
character-by-character proofreading. Apparent printed errors are distinguished
from OCR corrections in the chapter notes. English translations were prepared
for this edition; original figure labels remain Chinese.
