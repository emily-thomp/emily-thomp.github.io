# Introduction

Welcome to **Emily's Notes**.

This site presents openly readable technical literature, organizing its text, equations, tables and illustrations into chapters for reading, searching and comparison with the original sources.

Documents and notes are grouped under [Communications](communications/index.md), [Chemical Engineering](chemical-engineering/index.md), [Mechanical Engineering](mechanical-engineering/index.md), [Information Security](information-security/index.md) and [Artificial Intelligence](artificial-intelligence/index.md). The nitric acid handbook is in Chemical Engineering, and the report on model ideological alignment is in Artificial Intelligence.

Scanned books can be difficult to search, and their equations and tables inconvenient to reference. The site preserves the source material while providing searchable text and an English translation, so readers in different languages can explore the same document.

## Literature and notes

### Nitric Acid Handbook

*Nitric Acid Production by Ammonia Oxidation Using Locally Made Equipment*, compiled by Wu Yayi and published by Keji Weisheng Publishing House in December 1958.

The book describes the historical production of nitric acid through catalytic ammonia oxidation using local materials and small-scale equipment. It covers the flowsheet, equipment construction, operating controls, analysis, technical and economic indicators, catalyst manufacture and worker protection, alongside the industrial context of its period.

[Read the handbook](book/01-overview.md). Its contents are organized into eight chapters. Begin with the overview, or select Chemical Engineering in the top navigation and choose a chapter from the book's sidebar.

### Artificial Intelligence: Ideological alignment in open models

A public-information report using the Tankie series as a case study, covering synthetic data, fine-tuning, weight merging and hardware requirements. It distinguishes published records from inference and does not include inference tests of the target models.

[Read the ideological alignment report in Chinese](artificial-intelligence/ideological-alignment.md)

## Reading the site

- **Browse chapters:** select a subject in the top navigation, then use the books and chapters on the left and the section contents on the right.
- **Find a topic:** use the search box to locate terms or passages.
- **Change language:** the header language menu switches between Chinese and English while retaining the current chapter.
- **Check the original:** scan links at the end of each chapter allow comparison of text, equations and illustrations.

## Sources and corrections

The current edition has received initial proofreading and may still contain recognition or translation errors. Chinese text and SVG labels use simplified characters; original scan images are unchanged. Apparent original printing errors are retained with editorial notes, as are the original historical context and operating practices.

Use the repository link to inspect the source text and editorial records. To suggest a correction, open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/emily-thomp/emily-thomp.github.io/issues) with the chapter, original page number and proposed change.
