## Available literature

### Nitric Acid Handbook

*Nitric Acid Production by Ammonia Oxidation Using Locally Made Equipment*, compiled by Wu Yayi, Keji Weisheng Publishing House, December 1958.

The book covers production flowsheets, equipment, process control and analysis, catalyst manufacture and worker protection. Eight chapters are available in simplified Chinese and English, with SVG redraws and original scans for comparison.
